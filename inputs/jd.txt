Junior AI Engineer — Northwind Labs, Karachi (0–2 years experience)

About the role:
Northwind Labs is hiring a Junior AI Engineer to join a small team building
LLM-powered internal tools. You'll work across the stack: designing prompts,
building retrieval pipelines, wiring up APIs, and shipping features end to end
under senior review.

Must-have skills:
- Python fundamentals (data structures, OOP basics, writing clean functions)
- Experience with an LLM framework — LangChain or LangGraph preferred
- Understanding of RAG: chunking strategy, embeddings, retrieval quality,
  and how to evaluate whether retrieved context is actually relevant
- Comfortable with Git (branches, PRs, resolving conflicts) and consuming
  REST APIs
- At least one shipped project you can explain end to end — architecture,
  trade-offs, what broke, what you'd do differently

What we assess in the interview:
- Technical depth (can you go beyond the surface-level explanation?)
- Decomposition (can you break an ambiguous problem into steps?)
- Shipping ability (did you actually finish something, not just prototype it?)
- Debugging (how do you approach something that's broken?)
- Communication (can you explain technical decisions to a non-expert?)

Nice-to-haves:
- Experience with vector databases (FAISS, Chroma, pgvector, etc.)
- Exposure to evaluation/testing for LLM pipelines
- Familiarity with Docker and basic deployment
